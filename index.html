    How my work fits together

    I work across the whole AI stack, from data to infrastructure to the products people use, so my projects are drawn as one AI system: unstructured data feeds the systems (RAG + MLOps, and Sailor for LLM training), which run on infrastructure (Ray, GPUs and cloud) and power a solution (Fitness RAG). Underneath is me: I build the systems, give talks, and keep learning through ongoing projects.

    Research · SOSP '25

    Sailor: LLM training at scale

    ETH Zürich master's thesis: I designed and built Sailor's elastic, fault-tolerant runtime, which keeps training going on spot instances with in-CPU checkpoints (back to training 52 s after a preemption, 2.87× higher goodput than state of the art). I also worked on its resource planner.

    PyTorch · DeepSpeed · GKE · Distributed training

    Case study → Paper (SOSP '25) ↗ Code ↗

    Side project · 2025–2026

    RAG for fitness exercises

    A retrieval system over 873 exercises: hybrid BM25 + dense retrieval fused with RRF, contrastive fine-tuning of the embedder (Recall@10 0.36 → 0.81), MLflow tracking, Milvus Lite and a local LLM for grounded answers, all measured with a 69-query labelled eval set.

    RAG · Hybrid search · Embeddings · MLflow · Ollama
